Why SpeakLog

What learners lack most is speaking.

Many people wait until they're ready and can say it right, so they never say it at all. Those who do speak are left with nothing afterwards: no idea what went wrong, and nothing to show for it.

01

Just talk, no script

Record a quick take, or import a vlog or talking-head video you've already shot. No script, no waiting until you're "ready".

02

Compare the original and revision

Raw recognition is retained separately from reviewed and natural wording. Compare the source recording with your practice record to revisit your expression and later edits.

03

Polished, then published

Your original plus the improved version is a genuine study record. Export it as subtitles, a blog post or a video for the platforms you already use.

How it works

Four steps from a recording to a finished piece.

  1. Speak

    Drag in a voice memo, vlog or talking-head video, or hit record in the app. Imported files are referenced at their existing location.

  2. Transcribe

    On-device speech recognition with per-word timestamps and confidence, split into sentences at pauses. Speed depends on hardware, language and media.

  3. Review

    AI flags misheard words and unnatural phrasing, with reasons. Accept, skip or edit each sentence from the keyboard.

  4. Publish

    Export subtitles, a study blog post and a subtitle video — in any order, or just the one you need.

Two subtitle layers

What you said, and how a native speaker would say it — side by side.

  • As said only fixes what the machine misheard. Your grammar mistakes stay, so it reflects your real level.
  • Natural rewrites awkward phrasing the way a native speaker would, with a category and reason for every change.
  • Explanations are written in your own language, and the depth adapts to your level (CEFR, JLPT, HSK, TOPIK…).
  • Read the improved script from a teleprompter and re-record; subtitles align automatically.
As said

昨日は友達と新しいカフェを行きました。

Natural

昨日、友達と新しいカフェに行きました。

ParticleA destination takes に or へ; を marks a place you pass through or leave.
As said

ケーキがすごいおいしいでした。

Natural

ケーキがすごくおいしかったです。

ConjugationThe past of an i-adjective is 〜かった, not 〜でした. Use すごく to modify an adjective.

FAQ

Questions you might have

Which languages does SpeakLog support?

Practice languages are set per project, including English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, French, Spanish and German. Transcription depends on macOS speech support and may need a model download on first use. Explanation and translation languages are set separately.

Are my recordings and videos uploaded?

Transcription, rendering, face blurring and export run locally. Cloud AI sends the text and context each feature needs. Visual analysis passes sampled frames or photos to Claude Code or Codex; it still uses Codex when the global provider is the on-device model. Check automatic review and provider settings before using private content.

Will the AI overwrite what I actually said?

Transcription and natural wording are separate layers, and review lets you accept, skip or edit suggestions. The AI assistant and external agents can edit subtitles and other project content, so separate layers do not make every field immutable. Keep the original media and review important changes.

What do I need to run it?

The current 0.1.1 package requires an Apple silicon Mac (M-series chip) with macOS 26 or later. Intel Macs are not supported by this package. The on-device text AI also requires available Apple Intelligence.

Which formats can I export?

Export SRT, VTT or ASS subtitles; save Markdown or HTML blogs, copy rich text to note or Medium, or split text into an X thread. Video exports are MP4 in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1. You upload or publish the results yourself.
